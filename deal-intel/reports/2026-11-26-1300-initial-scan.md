## 2. Top deals accessible in Bangladesh
I could not confirm any Bangladeshi Black Friday campaign. Daraz BD's 11.11 sale ended on 21 Nov. Star Tech, Pickaboo and Ryans show 11.11 pages only. "Daraz Black Friday" codes on coupon sites are mostly Daraz **Pakistan** codes, so treat them as misleading.

What works from Bangladesh today:
- **Software and AI (no shipping, no customs):** Adobe CC Pro 57%, ElevenLabs 55%, Grammarly Pro 50% (V1). You need an international/dual-currency card with your annual USD endorsement available. Bangladeshi cards may also add a foreign-transaction fee (not determined).
- **Imported phones lose most of the discount.** Customs duty on finished handsets is 10% since Jan 2026, but the total tax burden is about **43.43%** (Daily Star, UNB). EST: Pixel 10 at $593 plus about $40 forwarding is about $633 assessed. Adding 43.43% gives about **$908 (≈ ৳111,000 at 122 BDT/USD)**. Compare that with the local market price before importing.
- **Laptops:** I did not find the import duty rate. One duty-free laptop is allowed **in passenger baggage** for personal use (commercial blog, unverified). If someone is travelling from the US, the MacBook Air at $1,049 becomes very attractive. Through a courier it is unknown until the NBR rate is checked.
